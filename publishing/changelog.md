# Initial alpha: AStages and Curios port

- Port the Pouch of Unknown to the listed Minecraft version and loader.
- Collect restricted items according to AStages pickup, inventory, and equipment restrictions.
- Add optional Curios pouch-slot support, configurable storage capacity, and unlocked-item retrieval.
- Preserve the original MIT license, attribution, and item artwork.
- Compiled and checked item packing; in-game behavior is not yet verified. The 1.21.1 artifact was compiled using a cached NeoForge development jar after a repository timeout.
